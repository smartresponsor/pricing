= Pricing Roadmap

== Milestone 1

* Price and PriceSet identity.
* External priceable-resource references.
* Currency and effective-date invariants.

== Milestone 2

* PriceList lifecycle.
* Contextual pricing rules.
* Deterministic selection explanations.

== Milestone 3

* Quantity tiers.
* Original/effective price semantics.
* Tax-inclusive metadata.

== Milestone 4

* Typed Cataloging, Retailing, Carting, and Ordering consumption contract.
* Currencing-backed supported-currency and precision semantics.
* Durable history, audit, provenance, and historical replay.
* Evidence-backed benchmark against Medusa v2, Vendure, and Sylius.
