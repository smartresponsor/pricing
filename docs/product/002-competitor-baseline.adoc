:revdate: 2026-09-21

This benchmark records only capabilities supported by local evidence or current official competitor documentation. Unsupported competitor conclusions remain NOT_VERIFIED.

== Boundary decision

Pricing should continue closing auditability, durable history, and typed neighbor-consumption contracts rather than absorbing promotions, tax calculation, FX, or order-total logic. Competitor capability alone is not a reason to duplicate another SmartResponsor component's ownership.
