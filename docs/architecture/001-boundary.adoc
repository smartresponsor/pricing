= Pricing Boundary

== Purpose

Pricing owns reusable price definitions and deterministic price selection independently of promotions, taxation, payments, and order totals.

== Owns

* price and price-set identity for external priceable resources
* price lists
* effective-date windows
* multi-currency price definitions
* contextual pricing rules
* deterministic best-price selection
* quantity and tier pricing
* original/reference versus effective price semantics
* tax-inclusive price metadata
* price history
* auditable selection explanations

== Does not own

* promotion eligibility, coupons, or campaigns; those belong to Promoting
* tax calculation; that belongs to Taxating
* currency registry or conversion; those belong to Currencing and Exchanging
* payments; those belong to Paying
* order aggregate totals; those belong to Ordering

== Current implementation scope

Pricing exposes immutable price, price-set, price-list, selection-criteria and selection-result contracts plus a deterministic selection service. Selection operates only on caller-provided reusable price definitions and performs no promotion evaluation, currency conversion, tax calculation, payment execution, or cart/order total aggregation.
